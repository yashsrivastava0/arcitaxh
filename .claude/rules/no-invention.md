# No invention

Applies everywhere. A fluent wrong answer is the worst failure this product can have.

- Do not invent prices, limits, rate tiers, model names or IDs, library versions, licences or competitor features.
- Versions come from the registry or the lockfile at build time. Vendor facts come from the vendor's own page, with the source recorded. Licences come from the package itself.
- If a fact cannot be checked, say so. Tag it Assumption or Open; never fill it in. Ask the founder.
- The worked numbers in the spec's Appendix C and the illustrative figures in the research report are placeholders, not facts. Never copy them into the KB, a prompt, a test expectation presented as real, or a user-facing string.
- Missing data makes a verdict `uncertain`; it never quietly removes an option and never becomes a pass.
- Never present a model's text as fact. Facts come from `kb/` records with a source, a quote, a verified date and an expiry.
