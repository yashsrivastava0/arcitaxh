# ADR-018: Credit ledger is first-party

Status: **Recommendation** (as recorded in specification v0.1, section 19).

## Context and Problem Statement

Mirrored from the decision log of [architecture specification v0.1](../01-source/architecture-specification-v0.1.pdf), section 19. Source: Research report.

Rationale given there: Payment providers' credit models reconcile late.

## Decision Outcome

Credit ledger is first-party. Proposed, not yet confirmed. Ask before changing it in a way that affects scope, cost or licences.
